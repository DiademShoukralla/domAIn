1domAIn redesign: foundations, every screen, key states
Foundations
1aLayout and navigation decisions
What changed and why
One top bar, everywhere. Mark on the left, a Chat / Connections switch in the middle, your account on the right. The switch is the only place the section name appears, so nothing is duplicated. With only two destinations, a segmented control is clearer than a tab bar, and keeping it at the top leaves the bottom of the screen to the composer and the keyboard.
Chat status is quiet when healthy. When the live connection drops, a strip appears directly above the composer saying what's happening and what happens to your message. The current state is also listed in the account menu.
Sources live above the input, not in it. "Answering from 3 sources" sits on its own line and opens Connections. The placeholder is short enough to never wrap.
The router is visible. Every reply is labelled Direct answer or Council review, so it's clear why some replies take longer.
Council progress is a timeline. Each seat has its own lane with four steps and a line saying what it's looking at right now. The chair waits until all three verdicts are in.
The chair owns the accent. In chat, the recommendation is the only amber-tinted surface, so the outcome stands apart from the three opinions.
The write-back plan never disappears. Refining, confirming, success and failure all happen inside the same card. An error explains itself and offers Retry, with the plan still below it.
Empty means guided. With no sources, chat shows how to connect one instead of a dead input.
1bApp mark
Three seats around one table. The dots sit in a slight arc, like chairs facing the viewer; the bar is the single recommendation they arrive at. Built from circles and one rounded bar, so it holds up at favicon size.

1dError wording
Raw responses are never shown. Each error maps to a sentence saying what happened, plus one next step.

Your session has ended. Sign in again to keep going.

5xx · council failed
The council couldn't finish this one. Your question is saved. Try again.
unknown
Something went wrong on our side. Try again, and if it keeps happening, let me know.
